# Fees and limits

## Card fees

- Debit card ( girocard ): free for all customers.
- Credit card: 29 EUR per year (retail), waived for premium from 4,000 EUR annual
  card spending, 99 EUR per year for business.
- Foreign ATM withdrawals outside the eurozone: 1.99% of the amount, minimum
  3.50 EUR, plus possible local ATM operator fees.
- ATM withdrawals inside the eurozone: free with the debit card.

## Transfer limits

- SEPA online transfer limit: 10,000 EUR per transaction and 25,000 EUR per day.
- Instant SEPA: up to 5,000 EUR per transaction, 24/7.
- International (SWIFT) transfer: 50,000 EUR per transaction; fee 0.25% of the
  amount, minimum 12 EUR, maximum 150 EUR. OUR/OUR SHA/BEN cost options are not
  supported in the demo.

## Card limits

- Debit card payment limit: 5,000 EUR per day.
- Credit card monthly limit: 3,000 EUR (retail), 5,000 EUR (premium/business).
  The current limit and the amount spent this month are available via the
  list_cards tool.
- Contactless payments do not count towards the daily payment limit.

## Deposit protection

Deposits are protected up to 100,000 EUR per customer per bank by the statutory
deposit guarantee scheme.
