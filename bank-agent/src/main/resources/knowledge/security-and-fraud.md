# Security and fraud

## If you suspect fraud

1. Freeze or block the affected card immediately (24/7 hotline 0800 123 4567).
2. Report the suspicious transaction in the app under "Report transaction".
3. Unauthorized card transactions are refunded in full once the investigation
   confirms the fraud; provisional credit is usually granted within 2 business
   days.

## Phishing and social engineering

- The bank never asks for your PIN, full card number, online banking password or
  one-time passwords (TAN) by phone, email or SMS.
- Always log in via the official app or by typing the bank's URL yourself.
- If you shared credentials after a suspicious call or message, change your
  password immediately and call the 24/7 hotline.

## Login and TAN

- Login uses username + password plus a TAN from the SecureGo app.
- The app shows a login notification for every new device; unknown devices can be
  revoked.
- After 5 failed login attempts the access is blocked for 24 hours.

## Data protection

- Transaction data is only shown to the authenticated customer.
- Documents uploaded in a chat are only visible in that chat; documents uploaded
  for "all my chats" are visible in all of that customer's conversations, never to
  other customers.
- Data is stored in the EU and encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
