# Cards

## Debit card (girocard)

- One debit card per checking account, free of charge.
- Contactless payments up to 50 EUR without PIN.
- Daily payment limit: 5,000 EUR. Monthly cash withdrawal limit at ATMs: 1,000 EUR
  in the eurozone (free), outside the eurozone see fees-and-limits.

## Credit card

- Annual fee: 29 EUR (retail), waived for premium customers from 4,000 EUR annual
  spending, 99 EUR (business).
- Monthly limit: 3,000 EUR (retail), 5,000 EUR (premium/business).
- Interest on the revolving balance: 14.9% p.a.; if the full statement amount is
  paid by direct debit, no interest is charged.
- Cash withdrawals with the credit card cost 4% of the amount, minimum 5 EUR.

## Card security features

- Cards can be temporarily frozen (lock) and unfrozen (unlock) in the app at any
  time, free of charge.
- Geo-blocking: payments outside the EU can be blocked on request.
- Every card payment sends a push notification.
- A card can be permanently blocked if lost or stolen; a replacement card is
  issued within 3-5 business days (free for the first replacement per year).
