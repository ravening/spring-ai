# Payments and transfers

## SEPA transfers

- Regular SEPA credit transfers arrive within one business day; submitted before
  16:00 on a business day they are usually executed the same day.
- Instant SEPA transfers arrive within seconds, 24/7, including weekends and
  holidays. Fee: 0.50 EUR per transfer.

## Standing orders

- Standing orders can be set up for weekly, monthly or yearly execution.
- They can be paused or deleted at any time free of charge.

## Direct debits

- SEPA direct debits can be returned within 8 weeks without giving a reason.
- After 8 weeks, returns are possible within 13 months if the debit was
  unauthorized.

## International payments

- SWIFT transfers usually take 1-3 business days depending on the destination
  country and correspondent banks.
- The exchange rate for non-EUR currencies is the ECB reference rate plus 1.5%.

## Spending categories

Transactions are categorized automatically: GROCERIES, DINING, TRANSPORT,
UTILITIES, ENTERTAINMENT, SHOPPING, HEALTH, SUBSCRIPTIONS, TRANSFER and INCOME.
The spending_summary and income_summary tools report totals per category for a
month.
