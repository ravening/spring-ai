# Accounts and products

## Everyday Checking (CHECKING)

- Free monthly account fee for retail customers; premium customers (bob) have the
  fee waived.
- Free SEPA transfers and direct debits in EUR.
- Overdraft (dispo) up to 2,000 EUR for retail, 5,000 EUR for premium, 10,000 EUR
  for business customers; overdraft interest is 9.9% p.a. (retail), 8.9% (premium),
  7.9% (business).
- Instant SEPA transfers cost 0.50 EUR per transfer if not included in the plan.

## Savings account (SAVINGS)

- Interest: 1.5% p.a. on balances up to 50,000 EUR, paid monthly.
- Withdrawals are free up to 2,000 EUR per calendar month; each additional
  withdrawal costs 2 EUR.
- No card is linked to the savings account.

## Investment account (INVESTMENT, business customers)

- Used for securities settlement; cash interest 0.5% p.a.
- Custody fee: 0.1% p.a. of assets under custody, minimum 4.90 EUR per month.

## Account statements

- Monthly PDF statement is available in the online banking inbox, free of charge.
- Additional paper statements cost 3.90 EUR each.
