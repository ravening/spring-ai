# Agent Labs Internal Handbook

This document is the internal reference for the Agent Labs demo organisation. It is
included in the agent's retrieval-augmented knowledge base so the assistant can answer
questions that no calculator, ticket or MCP tool can answer.

## Returns and refunds

Customers may return unused items within 45 days of delivery for a full refund. The
return window for opened software is 14 days. Refunds are issued to the original payment
method within 7 business days after the warehouse confirms receipt. The internal case
prefix for a return is `RET-`. To escalate a return, contact the `returns-ops` team.

## Support response targets

Priority CRITICAL issues get a first response within 15 minutes and a workaround within
4 hours. HIGH priority issues get a first response within 2 hours. MEDIUM issues get a
first response within 1 business day. The on-call rotation is called `sigma` and the
escalation bridge is named `bridge-alpha`. Tickets older than the target must be tagged
`breached` in the tracker.

## Expense policy

The standard travel meal allowance is 45 EUR per day within the EU and 60 USD per day
outside the EU. Any single expense above 250 EUR requires approval from the
`finance-approvers` group. Conference tickets up to 1500 EUR are pre-approved for
employees presenting a talk. The corporate travel portal is called `voyager`.

## Security and access

Production access requires a hardware security key and is granted through the
`vault-prod` group. Access reviews happen every 90 days. Any suspected credential leak
must be reported to `security-oncall` immediately and the incident codeword is
`red-lantern`. Never paste customer data into third-party demo tools.

## Onboarding

New engineers get a laptop on day one and are assigned an onboarding buddy from the
`platform-team`. The first-week checklist lives in the handbook under "first week" and
must be completed by the end of the fifth working day. Quarterly planning uses the
codename `north-star`.
